OFFICIAL SOURCE CHECK · 19 NOV 2026

Useful context.
Not a dataset.

The project cites Nepal Police CID’s Crime Monthly E-Bulletin, Year 9 Issue 4 (Shrawan 2083), for 1,567 registered Kathmandu Valley cases in that month. That is an aggregate monthly count—not incident rows with coordinates or time-of-day—so it cannot directly power the map or spatial models.

Read about the CID information unit ↗

The CID page describes monthly reporting to management; I could not locate a public downloadable row-level dataset in the pages checked. The cited bulletin figure should be verified against its issue before publication.
